## 2–3. Variants and the dev table
`cd $S && $R/.venv/bin/python $P/J72-16-scripts/expand16.py $P $S $P/cache/J72-16` (about 3 s)
- The recording walk, view B, the label fold, `lib.metrics` and the bootstrap are all expand.py's.
  - The fold is `J72-12 labels folded: 31 rows`. A `J72-16-labels.q*.jsonl` fold slot is ready and is 0 now.
  - The bootstrap is seed 20261023, 5 × choices(k=60), with B rescored on each resample.
  - Unjudged pairs count as negative.
- **Asserted on every run:**
  - Plain-prompt scores and weights equal `cache/dev-rows.json` bit for bit.
  - B == product `inject_candidates` on 60/60.
  - For E1, E2 and E3, the harness top 3 == product `inject_candidates(expanded query)` on 60/60 each.
- **Query construction.** Every added block is cut to 500 chars. The parts are joined with "\n", with empty blocks
  dropped.
- **E4** is `s(prompt) + λ·s(ctx)`. It is summed in the harness from two product walks, as B4 is, and an empty ctx
  gives `s = 0`.
- **E6** appends the previous prompt (cut to 500) to the prompt side when len(prompt) < 80, which is B3's rule. For an
  E4-type base, that means `s(prompt + prev1) + λ·s(ctx)`.
- **BEST rule** for E5 and E6, fixed in the script before the first run: max of (bar shape, boot passes, p@inject,
  r@3). It picked **E4 paths λ0.25** for both E5 and E6.

**Why it fails.**
- last_asst carries English, but it is long and topical to the *previous* turn. In BM25 it outweighs a short Thai
  prompt and drags in facts about that turn (E1: 45 rows changed, −15 judged positives).
- An E4 sum with any last_asst makes almost every fact score s > 0. Injected rises from 165 to 180 (always 3 per prompt),
  because the view-B gate admits s > 0. That alone costs precision.
- Paths are sparse (26/60 rows, median 0) and change little. E4 pa λ0.25 moves 10 rows with +0/−0 judged.

**Unjudged and bound.**
- The rule, top 3 E variants by lower-bound p, gives E6, E4 pa λ0.25 and E5. They have **11 distinct unjudged
  pairs in 10 prompts**, listed in `J72-16-unjudged.jsonl` as {id, name, variants}.
- Targets: `python3 -I $P/J72-16-scripts/targets16.py $P` gives `J72-16-judge-targets.q1.jsonl`, with 10 prompts,
  11 pairs and 25 KB.
  - It is J72-12's format: `copies()` imported from J72-2-rescore.py, `random.Random(20261026)`, ≤ 60 pairs per file.
  - It excludes dev-labels-merged `judged` and every J72-12 target.
  - `grep -c '"variant\|"rank\|"score'` gives 0. Two runs give the same sha1, 234ada00….
- **These 11 pairs cannot change the verdict at the measured rate.**
  - The measured rate is J72-15's judge of J72-12's expansion pairs, 15 positives of 67 = 0.224.
  - E4 pa λ0.25 and E5 cannot reach 0.3288 even with every pair positive (upper 0.3155).
  - E6 would need ≥ 6 of its 9 pairs positive (0.577). At 0.224 it lands at about 0.311, which is still below B3.
- E1, E3 and E4 both λ0.5 have the most headroom (breakeven 0.29–0.32). At 0.224 they land at p ≈ 0.30, below the
  0.3288 target. Their r@3 lower bound (0.26–0.33) sits far under 0.4242. They fall outside the brief's top-3 rule, so
  no targets were written for them.

**Choices made after seeing numbers.**
1. The unjudged/target set is restricted to E variants. The first run ranked B3 (a J72-12 reference with 0 unjudged)
   among the top 3. The restriction leaves the pair set unchanged, because E5's pairs are a subset of E4 pa's.
2. The breakeven column and the judged ± counts were added after the first run.

The BEST rule, the E6 reading ("B3's rule on top of BEST"), message grouping by `message.id`, CUT 500, SHORT 80,
λ ∈ {0.25, 0.5} and the 20-tool window were all fixed before any number existed.

## 4. Feasibility and latency: reading the tail of `transcript_path`
`python3 -I $P/J72-12-scripts/offsets.py ~/.claude/projects $P $S/offsets.jsonl && node $P/J72-16-scripts/lat_ctx.mjs $S/offsets.jsonl $P/cache/J72-16-ctx.jsonl`
- **Method.** For each dev prompt, the transcript ends at the prompt's record (offsets.py), which is the hook-time end
  of file. The script does one read of the last 256 KiB, drops the leading partial line, and parses main-chain
  assistant records by ctx.py's rules. Each row is repeated 21 times and the median is taken. Warm page cache,
  Node v25.2.1, read and parse only.
- **Cost: p50 0.49 ms, p90 0.54 ms, max 0.55 ms.** The read is bounded by construction, unlike J72-12's unbounded
  backward scan (max 220 ms).
- **Context inside the tail.**
  - last_asst matches the full-transcript answer on **60/60**.
  - paths match on **50/60**, so both match on 50/60.
  - In all 10 misses the tail's path list is a newest-first prefix of the full list (0–5 of 1–6 paths), because the
    last 20 tool_use blocks reach further back than 256 KiB.
  - 11 of 60 prompts sit within the first 256 KiB of their file.
- The `transcript_path` surface already exists (`HookInput.transcript_path`, runtime-ts/src/hookadapter.ts). Moot,
  because no variant passes. Both runtimes plus a conformance case would be needed, and none is proposed.
