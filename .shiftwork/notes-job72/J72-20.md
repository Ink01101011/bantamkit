**How to read it.**
- **Thai expansion text is the noise, as in probe A.** G1 and G3 (with Thai) fill all 180 slots and gather 20–57
  unjudged full-body facts. That is the same Thai-bigram overlap failure A had, but on a separate vector.
  - With Thai in the expansion doc, s_q2d is non-zero on a median of 179 rows per prompt.
  - With latin only (G5), the median is 14 rows, and 8 prompts get no s_q2d at all.
- **Latin-only expansions (G5) are the only clean signal.** They are at or slightly above B: +0.011 p and up to
  +0.041 r@3, while injecting few unjudged pairs (6–12).
- **The stub split goes against the brief's worry.** Stub facts carry most of the gain.
  - On stub facts, G5/G6 gain 2–6 judged positives at a constant injected count: B 28/95, G6 34/95.
  - On full-body facts, the extra injected pairs land on unjudged ones (8–11).
  - A stub is described only by its description, so the expansions add vocabulary there.
- **G4 (rerank only) makes no real gain:** p 0.2848, below G5-l0.25.

**Choices made after seeing numbers.**
- The stub rule (the first run assumed empty bodies and found none).
- The per-resample G6 breakdown and the already-targeted lookup.
- Nothing else. The grid, BEST rule, G4/G6 derivation and λ values were fixed in the docstring before the first run.

## Cost and feasibility
- **The sidecar.** Expansions would live in a derived sidecar keyed by fact text hash, because bantamkit cannot write
  native facts.
  - Size: 441 rows of `{h, x[6]}` JSONL come to **235,070 B** (about 533 B per fact).
  - For scale, the hot index is 1.32 MB (J72-11), so the sidecar is about +18% of the index if stored raw.
  - G6 uses only the latin tokens, so a latin-only sidecar would be smaller.
- **Hook cost of one more scored vector, UNINDEXED** (every expansion doc tokenized on every call, so an upper bound).
  - Command: `node $P/J72-20-scripts/lat20.mjs $S/lat-docs.json`. This is J72-17's lat17.mjs on probe20's dump:
    60 prompts × 21 reps, the median per prompt.
  - Node `bm25Scores`: p50 **2.65 ms** in both runs (2.654 and 2.660), p90 4.2 ms, max 16 ms.
  - Python: p50 32 ms, p90 46 ms.
  - A posting index over the sidecar would make this cost a fraction of that.
- **G6 also needs B3's head.** That means the previous prompt (transcript tail, p50 0.49 ms per J72-17) and a second
  pooled BM25 pass for prompts under 80 chars.
  - Against `hook_p50_rise_ms_max_embedding_off` 5.0, unindexed G6 is about 2.65 + 0.5 + one pooled pass.
  - That is tight. It needs measuring on the real hook before any claim.
- **Generating the expansions** is the host model at save time, with no ollama, which fits the user's ruling. But it
  cannot reach existing native facts without a backfill, and stubs are 28% of keys. Here J72-19's four blind
  generators wrote them.
