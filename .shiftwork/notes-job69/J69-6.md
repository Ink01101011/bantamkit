## Deviations (code wins; property kept)

1. **Event log not folded.** Plan: "hook-log and eventlog count by event/tool per day". The event log is opt-in per
   store, bounded by its own documented `.1` rotation (J69-4 D1, a cross-runtime contract), and `--mcp-report`'s
   `parse_event_line` counts a row with no `tool` as unreadable. Folding it would change that report; it is left out.
2. **Rotation = idle 30 days.** "gzips old segments" is read as: a live log whose mtime is older than the 30-day
   cutoff is gzipped into its next `.N.gz`. Rotated segments are never rewritten or re-folded.
3. **Usage fold at the cutoff, not now**, and only when an old non-save line exists (no-op second run; no fact
   made to look recently used). The fold keeps weights: the folded row is the weight at the cutoff, decayed by
   `_weights_of` from its ts.
4. **Report gains `compacted`** (names of cold indexes compaction rewrote). Additive.
5. **Roots = the session cwd exactly** (no walk-up to a parent `.shiftwork`): a walk-up from a test cwd inside this
   checkout would reach the checkout's own jobs.
6. **Job files** = checkpoint, `.log.jsonl`, `briefs-<job>/**`, `notes-<job>/**` (regular files; symlinks left and
   their dirs kept). `.shiftwork/briefs/` (no suffix) and old `jobN-*` dirs are not bound to any checkpoint and are
   never touched. The bare `checkpoint.json` is job `checkpoint`.
7. **Bundle encoding**: a non-UTF-8 file is `{"base64": …}` (plan: "utf8 text | base64 bytes"); strict UTF-8
   keeps a BOM as U+FEFF on both sides (Node `TextDecoder({fatal, ignoreBOM: true})`).
8. **`search` hits carry the 1-based line number**, not the line text; a hit needs every `tokenize_v2` term of the
   query on that line. Jobs come from the cold index (synced from `.shiftwork/archive/*.json.gz` first, keyed on
   the archive's relative path).
9. **Server stands down** when `~/.bantamkit/hooks/hook-log.jsonl` was written within 24 h (no new state file).
10. **No SessionStart log field** was added (the `hooks` suite compares those records); only a spawn failure warns.
11. **Child takes `<cwd> <home>`** (after the incident); `retention.json` also records `report` / `error`.
12. Python `maybe_spawn` leaves a never-waited `Popen` (a `ResourceWarning` only under `-W error`/dev mode).
